From coder to closer.

Since 2018, I've built complex technology products in high-stakes environments — finance and government — where "close enough" isn't good enough. Precision, security, and integrity weren't preferences; they were requirements.

In May 2026, I earned my Master's in Computer Science, where I studied coursework in advanced algorithms and machine learning. Today I apply that expertise to help clients make confident, informed real estate decisions.

Buying or selling a home involves real risk and countless variables. You deserve an agent with the experience to navigate the complexity, and the analytical training to model it before you sign.
