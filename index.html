About Me

From Coder to Closer

Why I Traded Software for Real Estate

Since 2018, I've built complex technology products in high-stakes environments like finance and government—where “close enough” isn't good enough. Precision, security, and integrity were requirements.

Buying or selling a home involves real risk and countless variables, and you deserve an agent who has the experience to navigate those complexities. I provide rigorous data analytics to power my negotiations, and I work tirelessly to guide my clients through the biggest transactions of their lives.

Being “data-driven” isn't a buzzword for me—it's a commitment. I create custom-built tools to evaluate market trends, inform pricing strategy, and maximize offer strength, providing you clear, defensible guidance instead of guesswork.

I've worked as a data scientist and senior software engineer for Fortune 500 companies, and in May 2026 I will earn my Master in Computer Science, studying advanced algorithms and machine learning. Today, I apply my expertise to help real estate clients make confident, informed decisions.

My promise

I bring the same rigor from my software career to your real estate journey, advocating for your best interests with integrity, backed by data you can trust.
