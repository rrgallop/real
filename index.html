Applied to real estate.

A transaction most agents handle on intuition, I treat as a system.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We start with a structured conversation about timing, priorities, and constraints. The plan is built around your goals, not the market's noise.

  2. 02

    Quantitative Analysis

    Custom-built pricing models, hand-curated comparables, and scenario analyses replace the gut-feel approach you'll find elsewhere. You see the math, not just the conclusion.

  3. 03

    Defensible Positioning

    Every offer or list price is backed by a written rationale you can defend in any conversation — whether at the negotiation table or with your spouse over dinner.

  4. 04

    Disciplined Execution

    Inspections, contingencies, lender coordination, escrow. Transactions fail at the seams. I treat the close as a system with checkpoints, not a sequence of phone calls.

From coder to closer.

Since 2018, I've built complex technology products in high-stakes environments — finance and government — where "close enough" isn't good enough. Precision, security, and integrity weren't preferences; they were requirements.

In May 2026, I earned my Master's in Computer Science, where I studied coursework in advanced algorithms and machine learning. Today I apply that expertise to help clients make confident, informed real estate decisions.

Buying or selling a home involves real risk and countless variables. You deserve an agent with the experience to navigate the complexity, and the analytical training to model it before you sign.
